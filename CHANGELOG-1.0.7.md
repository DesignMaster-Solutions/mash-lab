## 1.0.7

- Fix: on some browsers the very first frame arrived with a negative time step and the title screen froze before you could tap. Time never runs backwards now, and a bad frame can no longer stop the game.
- The Monster Mash: every delivered monster now does a little dance on the table and struts off to the villager, and the next one pops in. The lantern waits while it dances.
- A miss makes the monster fall apart (head one way, extra the other) and stitch itself back together.
- First orders of every run: the part boxes that bring a trait you still need glow in that trait's color, so the color code teaches itself.
- New key art for the jam thumbnail, drawn by the game itself.
